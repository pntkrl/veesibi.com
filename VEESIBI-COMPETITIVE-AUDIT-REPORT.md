## STEP 1 — PRODUCT IDENTIFICATION

### What Problem Does VEESIBI Solve?
VEESIBI solves the problem: **"Am I visible when AI answers questions about my category?"** It measures a domain's AI search visibility across 10 sub-scores (crawlability, llms.txt compliance, entity authority, structured data, trust, multi-engine citations, GEO share of voice, content density, ranking position, and AI technical readiness), generates an overall 0–100 score with letter grade, and provides one-click remediation code for llms.txt, robots.txt, and JSON-LD schema.

### Ideal User
- **Primary:** SaaS founders, solo marketers, indie hackers who want to know if ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini cite them
- **Secondary:** SEO/marketing agencies managing multiple client domains
- **Tertiary:** Enterprise brand teams monitoring AI visibility

### Market Category
**GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) / AI Visibility Audit Tools** — a nascent, rapidly growing sub-category of SEO tools born from the shift to AI-powered search.

### Search Intent Satisfies
- "Check if my site appears in AI search"
- "llms.txt validator"
- "AI search visibility score"
- "Am I cited by ChatGPT"
- "GEO audit tool"
- "AI crawler permissions check"

### Likely Target Keywords
`AI visibility scorecard`, `llms.txt validator`, `GEO tool`, `AI search audit`, `ChatGPT citation check`, `AI crawler audit`, `generative engine optimization`, `answer engine optimization`, `AI SEO tool`, `llms.txt generator`, `robots.txt AI bots`
