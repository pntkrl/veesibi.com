1. Product Identification

What Problem Does VEESIBI Solve?

VEESIBI solves: "Am I visible when AI answers questions about my category?" It measures a domain's AI search visibility across 10 sub-scores (crawlability, llms.txt compliance, entity authority, structured data, trust, multi-engine citations, GEO share of voice, content density, ranking position, and AI technical readiness), generates an overall 0–100 score with letter grade, and provides one-click remediation code for llms.txt, robots.txt, and JSON-LD schema.

Ideal User

  • Primary: SaaS founders, solo marketers, indie hackers — "Does ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini cite me?"
  • Secondary: SEO/marketing agencies managing multiple client domains
  • Tertiary: Enterprise brand teams monitoring AI visibility

Market Category

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) / AI Visibility Audit Tools — a nascent, rapidly growing sub-category of SEO tools born from the shift to AI-powered search.

Search Intent

"Check if my site appears in AI search", "llms.txt validator", "AI search visibility score", "Am I cited by ChatGPT", "GEO audit tool", "AI crawler permissions check"

Target Keywords

AI visibility scorecard, llms.txt validator, GEO tool, AI search audit, ChatGPT citation check, AI crawler audit, generative engine optimization, answer engine optimization, AI SEO tool, llms.txt generator, robots.txt AI bots

12. Brutal Critique

Pretending I invested $10 million into this startup:

What Feels Unfinished

Everything. The hash-based scoring makes the core product a lie. The citation matrix fabricates data. There's no real AI testing happening. The dashboard is a shell. The monitoring panel shows nothing meaningful. The score trend chart has no data. This feels like a weekend hackathon project that got a nice coat of Vercel-inspired paint.

What Feels Amateur

  • The "VEESIBI v1.0" badge — signals beta/unfinished
  • The live audit ticker with fabricated scores — signals dishonesty
  • The "Claim Domain" CTA — confusing, doesn't communicate value
  • Zero testimonials, zero social proof — signals no one uses this
  • No blog, no content — signals no investment in growth
  • No Product Hunt launch — signals the founder isn't serious about distribution
  • The JSON-LD generator that doesn't generate valid schema on its own site

What Destroys Trust

  1. The citation matrix showing real AI engine citations that are completely fabricated
  2. The hash-based scoring presenting random numbers as meaningful analysis
  3. No llms.txt on the site that validates llms.txt
  4. No JSON-LD on the site that generates JSON-LD
  5. The live ticker showing fake "live" scores

What Users Will Hate

  • Discovering the scores are random/hash-based
  • The "Claim Domain" flow leading to a signup wall
  • No way to export or share reports professionally
  • No email alerts when their score changes
  • No real competitor comparison

What Users Will Love

  • The llms.txt validator — genuinely useful and well-built
  • The 1-click remediation code generation
  • The clean Vercel-inspired design
  • The instant audit concept (when it works)
  • The embeddable badge concept
  • The pSEO comparison and industry pages

What Prevents Going Viral

  • No viral mechanics beyond the badge (requires 85+ score)
  • No sharing features (no social buttons, no OG images)
  • No leaderboard or public rankings
  • No embeddable widgets other than badge
  • No API for developers
  • No "wow" moment that makes people share

What Prevents Recommendations

  • Zero trust signals — no one recommends a tool with no testimonials
  • Fabricated data — users will warn others once they discover
  • No comparison with competitors — can't justify switching
  • No free tier depth — 3 audits/month too limiting
  • No PDF reports for clients (agency case dead)

What Prevents Backlinks

  • No content to link to — no blog, no research, no guides
  • No unique data or insights — no reason for journalists to cite
  • No embeddable tools beyond badge
  • No API that developers would document
  • No open-source components

What Prevents Product Hunt Success

  • No clear "before/after" or "wow" demo
  • The product is a tool, not a platform — limited PH appeal
  • No existing community to mobilize for upvotes
  • The hash-based scoring would be exposed in comments
  • No hunter with audience

What Prevents SEO Growth

  • Zero content — the single biggest blocker
  • No backlinks — domain authority too low to rank
  • No comparison pages with real differentiation
  • No educational content for informational queries
  • No FAQ or schema markup on own site

What Prevents Long-Term Retention

  • No reason to come back — audit once and done
  • No scheduled monitoring (free tier)
  • No email alerts
  • No dashboard value without claimed domains
  • No community or ecosystem

Can This Realistically Become #1?

No — not in its current state.

1. The core product is a lie. Hash-based scoring and fabricated citation data mean the tool doesn't actually do what it claims.

2. Trust is at zero. No testimonials, no social proof, no content, no directory listings. In a market where Profound has $155M and Peec has 347% YoY growth, VEESIBI is invisible.

3. No competitive moat. The only differentiation is "instant, free, unauthenticated" — but the instant results are fake.

4. No distribution strategy. No Product Hunt, no content, no directories, no community.

How It Could Become #1

  1. Build real AI engine testing — Query ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude APIs. This is the #1 priority.
  2. Position as the "free alternative" — Own the free tier in a $499/mo market.
  3. Lean into llms.txt validator — Best implementation in market. Make it the best llms.txt tool, then expand.
  4. Launch on Product Hunt — The llms.txt validator alone could get 500+ upvotes.
  5. Publish GEO research — Otterly's llms.txt experiment generated massive backlinks.
  6. Build embeddable score widget — Every embed = backlink + awareness.
